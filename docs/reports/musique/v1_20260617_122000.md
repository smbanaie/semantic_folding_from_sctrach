## Analysis

### 1. BM25 Dominates on Multi-hop QA
BM25 outperforms SF on all metrics by 30-44%. MuSiQue requires composing facts across 2-5 hops, which favors precise lexical matching over semantic similarity.

### 2. SF struggles with compositional queries
47.7% of queries had no gold passage in the top results. Multi-hop queries require matching specific entity combinations that SF's semantic approach conflates.

### 3. Pattern Across All Benchmarks

| Dataset | Task Type | SF MRR | BM25 MRR | Gap |
|---------|-----------|--------|----------|-----|
| PubMedQA | Biomedical QA | 0.969 | 1.000 | -3.1% |
| PopQA | Entity lookup | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0% |
| NarrativeQA | Script comprehension | 0.939 | 0.980 | -4.2% |
| Belebele | Reading comprehension | 0.880 | 0.995 | -11.6% |
| NQ-REaR | Factoid retrieval | 0.574 | 0.638 | -10.0% |
| **MuSiQue** | **Multi-hop QA** | **0.453** | **0.672** | **-32.6%** |

**Pattern**: SF excels on domain-specific tasks (biomedical) and simple lookup. Performance degrades on multi-hop reasoning where lexical precision matters.

## Recommendations

1. **Use BM25 for MuSiQue** — 32.6% MRR advantage
2. **SF strength is domain-specific** — PubMedQA (0.969) remains its best result
3. **Hybrid SF+BM25** may help on paraphrased queries (tested on Belebele: +2.0%)
